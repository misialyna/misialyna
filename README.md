## Research & engineering

### MSc research — Information embedding in generative audio *(ongoing)*

My thesis research explores **semantic steganography and watermarking in synthetic audio**, focusing on token-level information embedding in autoregressive audio generation. I am investigating an **ARCDStego–Qwen** approach, including token probabilities, stateful generation, decoding requirements, and robustness evaluation.

The work is **in progress**: the current focus is on experimental methodology, implementation and validation. I do not present the method as a finished or independently validated system.

### [AIWAT / WATUŚ — Speech interaction subsystem](https://github.com/misialyna/watus_project)

I am responsible for the voice interaction subsystem of an autonomous AI assistant developed in a student research team at WAT. My work covers:

- **Speech recognition:** Whisper / Faster-Whisper, Polish-language configuration, voice activity detection.
- **Speaker recognition:** SpeechBrain / ECAPA-TDNN and speaker-aware dialogue handling.
- **Speech synthesis:** Evaluation and integration of neural TTS systems including Piper.
- **Real-time engineering:** Edge inference, latency measurement and event-driven integration with other assistant components using ZeroMQ.

AIWAT received a **Rector's Award** and **1st place at the Students' Cybernetics Symposium (2026)**. The public repository linked above is a project reference; not all ongoing development is publicly available.

### [STT/TTS Benchmark — Open-source evaluation platform](https://github.com/misialyna/stt-tts-benchmark)

I designed and built a platform for comparing speech-to-text and text-to-speech solutions using reproducible workloads and measurable metrics. It supports cross-provider evaluation, a browser interface, a Python backend and exportable results.

The evaluation workflow includes **WER, inference latency and real-time performance**. The results informed technology choices for AIWAT.
